\section{A polynomial integral}
For a positive limit $a>0$, the integral in \eqref{eq:integral} has a closed form.
\begin{equation}
  J(a)=\int_0^a x^2\,dx=\frac{a^3}{3}.
  \label{eq:integral}
\end{equation}
\section{A matrix example}
Let $v=(x,y)^\top$. A diagonal map scales its second coordinate.
\begin{equation}
  A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix},
  \qquad Av=\begin{bmatrix}x\\2y\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
